FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Some useful information for visitors to your website.

Are the guides free?

You can make your guides freely accessible. Add your own terms if you offer downloads or services.

Will these tweaks increase my FPS?

Results depend on the hardware, game and settings. Some changes may have no measurable effect. Avoid promising specific performance gains without testing.

How do I undo a system change?

Document each change and provide clear instructions for restoring the original settings. Make a backup or restore point before significant modifications.

Can I use these guides on any PC?

Check the Windows version and hardware requirements for each guide. Not every setting applies to every PC.
